# PSX Furniture Free

**36 free PS1-style props to furnish a room**: a bed, a wardrobe and a sofa, a kitchen corner whose fridge, stove and cupboards open, a TV, a phone, and the boxes, cans and newspapers that make it lived in. Low-poly and point-filtered, a scene per prop, ready to drop in. Free for commercial use.

### Want more?

**[PSX Furniture](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-furniture)** has 476 props and 157 animations:
beds, sofas, tables and chairs by the dozen, whole kitchens and bathrooms, washing machines, fans,
CRTs, record players and rotary phones, boxes, books, bottles, toys and junk, and a Unity package.
Same addon, same look: it installs over this one.

For the flat around them, [PSX Apartment Kit Free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-apartment-kit-free)
has 15 modular pieces on the same grid (papered walls, a door, a sash window), free too.
For what's cooked in the kitchen, [PSX Kitchen and Food Free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-kitchen-and-food-free)
has 15 more: a range that lights, a microwave, a rotten steak with its flies.

### What's inside

- **Bedroom:** a single bed, a nightstand and a wardrobe that open.
- **Living room:** a sofa, an armchair, a coffee table, a floor lamp and a table lamp, a rug, a
  bookcase, a CRT television, a rotary phone.
- **Dining:** a pine table, a ladder-back chair and a broken one.
- **Kitchen:** a base unit, a sink unit and a wall cabinet that open, a stove and a fridge that
  open, a kettle, a mug, a plate, soup, cereal, milk and a beer can.
- **Clutter:** a cardboard box and an open one, a pile of books, a newspaper, a trash bag, a
  picture and a wall clock that ticks.
- **Room:** a papered wall and a board floor in 2 m pieces to set them in.
- **13 animations** on the moving parts.
- **36 icons**, 256x256 PNGs with transparency, rendered from the models.
- 8 to 304 triangles a prop, one 1024x1024 texture atlas, shading baked into the vertex colours,
  real-world scale.

### Godot 4.3 to 4.7

An addon with a scene per prop: box colliders on every part (the moving ones move with them), an
AnimationPlayer where it moves, one lit material so your flashlight falls on them, and a gallery.
Tested on Godot 4.3 and 4.7, on Forward+, Mobile and Compatibility.

```gdscript
# fridge_white: the prop in your level
fridge_white.get_node("AnimationPlayer").play("open")
```

For the full PS1 look, [PSX Look](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look) turns the props (and your
level) over in one line: wobbling vertices, warping textures, 240p, dither and 15-bit colour. Its
[free sampler](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-look-free) has the vertex snap and affine textures.

For the sound of it, [PSX Horror SFX](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-sfx) has footsteps
on concrete, tile and metal, creaking doors, creatures, stingers and the typewriter save,
PS1-style ([30 free](https://heyheythere.itch.io/psx-horror-sfx-free)).
